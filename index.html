A SURVIVAL GUIDE

Win the crowd.
Keep your head.

Travel through four story chapters and defeat twelve named rivals. Visit Brann’s Forge to buy and equip gear, Mira’s tavern for rumors and jobs, and Cassian’s yard for training. Each move you make gives your opponent a turn. Read their next move above the arena before you act.

  • Quick strike: accurate, free attack.
  • Heavy blow: stronger, but can miss. Costs 3 energy.
  • Guard: blocks 70% of the next hit and restores 2 energy.
  • Catch breath: restores 4 energy, but leaves you open.
  • Glory strike: powerful and accurate. Costs 5 energy; three-turn cooldown.
  • Bandage: heals up to 30 health. Two per fight.

Victories earn gold and XP. Buy gear between fights. Health, energy, and bandages refill for every bout. Defeat costs no gold—you can retry. Progress saves between bouts in this browser.
